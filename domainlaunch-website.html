FAQs

Good to know

A few answers to common domain questions.

Will I own a domain I buy?

For an outright purchase, the intended process is to transfer the domain registration to you or your business. The registrant details and transfer completion should be checked before treating the sale as complete.

Is leasing the same as owning?

No. In a lease arrangement, the domain registrant usually remains the domain owner and you receive only the usage rights set out in the written agreement. The terms should explain payment, renewal, cancellation and what happens at the end.

Can you manage a domain I already own?

Yes, subject to agreeing the tasks and access needed. You remain the registrant, and the service should clearly state who is responsible for renewals, DNS changes and approvals.

Are the prices fixed?

The figures on this page are illustrative starting prices. The final quote depends on the domain, third-party costs and the service requested. You should receive the full price before agreeing.

Do I have to go ahead after making an enquiry?

No. An enquiry is just a way to discuss your requirements. No purchase, transfer or paid service should begin until the details and terms have been agreed.
